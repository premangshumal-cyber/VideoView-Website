Playback engine

Get lost in the scene.
Stay in control.

From everyday files to more demanding media, VideoView puts essential playback controls where you need them.

Responsive playback controls

Seek, pause, resume, stop, jump 10 seconds and choose from eleven playback-speed settings up to 4×.

Frame it your way

Fullscreen, aspect/fit/original sizing, zoom, and rotation controls for different viewing styles.

Audio your way

Volume, mute, multiple audio tracks where present, and device preferences.

More accessible captions

Subtitle tracks and external files, offset controls and styling choices.

Keyboard-first actions

Playback and navigation shortcuts, with a reference to help you learn the controls.

Renderer options

Automatic or D3D output preferences, and hardware decoding choices where available.

The playlist workspace

Queue multiple files, browse nested folders, drag to reorder, find by name and sort by metadata without leaving your session.

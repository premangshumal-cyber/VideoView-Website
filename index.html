Why VideoView

Everything you need. Nothing in your way.

A thoughtful set of playback tools, presented in a calm, beautiful desktop experience.

01

A picture worth playing

Play local videos and audio with a LibVLC-based engine, hardware-related rendering options, and flexible view controls.

02

Playlists, perfected

Build a queue, search and sort your media, reorder tracks, and keep the next great thing ready.

03

Every word in sync

Open external subtitle files, adjust delays, and tune subtitle appearance to your preferences.

04

Settings that fit you

Control speed, renderer choices, zoom, aspect ratio, rotation, and audio device preferences.

05

Made for your desktop

Go full screen, switch to a mini window, and use keyboard shortcuts to stay in control.

06

Your library, your files

Import media files or folders, keep your playback history, and use recent-item resume features.

*Actual codec support depends on the bundled VLC plugins. Windows operation remains pending native acceptance testing.
